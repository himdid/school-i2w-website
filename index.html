Fresh Bread for the Neighborhood

Welcome to North Star Bakery! We bake fresh bread, rolls, and morning pastries every day for our local community. Whether you need a quick coffee and roll on your morning commute or a loaf of sourdough for dinner, we have you covered.

What We're Known For

We are known for our crusty sourdough bread, French baguettes, and morning sweet rolls. We make our dough from scratch every morning using simple ingredients without extra additives.
