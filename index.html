Welcome to North Star Bakery

Freshly Baked for Our Community

Welcome to your neighborhood corner bakery! Every morning before dawn, our ovens are fired up to bring you warm, handmade breads, flaky pastries, and artisanal baked goods crafted with local ingredients and care.

What We're Known For

We specialize in naturally leavened sourdough loaves, slow-fermented crusty baguettes, and delicate laminated morning buns. Made using time-honored traditional techniques with zero artificial preservatives.

Featured Item of the Week

Our Baker's Reserve Cardamom Morning Buns—rolled with stone-ground organic flour, infused with fresh crushed cardamom, and dusted in cane sugar.
