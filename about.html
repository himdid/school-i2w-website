About North Star Bakery

Our Story

Founded in 2018, North Star Bakery started as a simple home kitchen operation delivering fresh sourdough boules to our neighborhood farmers market. As our community grew, so did our ambition to open a permanent brick-and-mortar home where locals, commuters, and families can gather over good food and honest baking.

Behind the Scenes

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great bread starts with soil health. We partner directly with regional grain farmers who use regenerative agriculture techniques. Every flour blend we use is 100% unbleached, unbromated, and stone-ground to retain its natural wheat germ, oils, and nutrients. Our butter, eggs, and seasonal fruits come exclusively from local farms within 100 miles of our bakery.

Meet the Bakers

Elena Ramos — Head Baker & Founder

Trained in traditional French sourdough baking, Elena spent a decade working in European wood-fired bakeries before bringing her craft back home to open North Star.

Marcus Vance — Pastry Chef

Marcus heads our morning pastry program, meticulously laminating croissant doughs and pairing seasonal jams with sweet cream fillings.
