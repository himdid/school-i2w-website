About Our Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2018 when we were just selling a few dozen loaves of bread each weekend at the local farmers market. People kept asking where they could buy our bread during the week, so we finally found a small storefront downtown and opened our doors to the neighborhood.

Behind the Scenes

Where We Get Our Ingredients

Good bread does not need complicated ingredients. We source our flour from regional grain mills, and we buy our dairy and fruit from farms within an hour of our shop whenever possible. We never use artificial dough conditioners or preservatives in anything we sell.
